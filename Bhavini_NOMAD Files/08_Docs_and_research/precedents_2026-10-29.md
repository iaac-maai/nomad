# Precedents and temporary housing: what exists, with sources (29 Oct 2026)

Answer to Critic A ("were there other people who have done that?") and Critics A and C (temporary / emergency housing).
Every number below is copied from the source named next to it. **Secondary** = press or a company website, not the
public body itself: use it as an order of magnitude and confirm with the official data before putting it on a slide.

**What it means for NOMAD**
- Barcelona converts about 70 to 100 shops a year (secondary figures), with 6,149 empty. The bottleneck is the
  process, not the stock: NOMAD's job is to say quickly which of the 6,149 are worth starting.
- **Madrid's Plan RESIDE protects shops on commercial streets.** That is the same idea as NOMAD's "keep it a shop" answer,
  so a real city already applies this logic.
- **England checks natural light as a condition of the conversion route.** That is the same problem as the "jail" plans
  Critic B described, and a precedent for adding Bars's daylight check to NOMAD's answer.

## 2. Temporary / emergency housing in Barcelona already exists

| What | Number | Source (type) |
|---|---|---|
| APROP (Allotjaments de Proximitat Provisionals): homes from recycled shipping containers, for families with children at risk of exclusion, with a social-services plan, so they can stay in their neighbourhood | Gòtic (Nou de Sant Francesc): 12 homes; Glòries: 42 homes on 7 floors, built in 26 weeks, €5.77 M, about 100 people; total 54 | Ajuntament de Barcelona, Drets Socials (official) — https://ajuntament.barcelona.cat/dretssocials/es/innovacion-social/aprop ; barcelona.cat (official) — https://www.barcelona.cat/infobarcelona/ca/tema/habitatge/enllestit-el-segon-bloc-dallotjaments-industrialitzats-aprop-a-les-glories_1239068.html ; idealista, 27 Oct 2026 (secondary) for the total of 54 |

**What it means for NOMAD:** the city has spent €5.77 M on 42 temporary homes. A converted empty shop gives a temporary
home with no new building and no new land. That is a comparison worth showing, but we have no conversion cost per
home yet. NOMAD only asks the user for a cost per m², so we can't claim it is cheaper until we have a real cost source.

## 3. Is there a legal route for *temporary* housing in a shop?

What we found (not yet read in full; these must be downloaded and added to the rulebook before NOMAD uses them):
- **Llei 18/2007** (in our rulebook) defines *allotjament dotacional*: accommodation for people's temporary housing needs.
  It can be built on land zoned as a community-facility system (equipament). This is about land for facilities, not about
  ground-floor shops.
- **Decret llei 2/2025, de 25 de febrer** and **Llei 11/2025, de 29 de desembre** (Catalonia) changed Llei 18/2007 and
  the planning law. Whether they open a route for temporary use of premises is **not confirmed**.
  ICAB copies: https://www.icab.es/export/sites/icab/.galleries/documents-noticies/Decret-llei-02_2025-de-25-de-febrer-de-mesures-urgents-en-materia-dhabitatge-i-urbanisme.pdf ;
  https://www.icab.es/export/sites/icab/.galleries/documents-noticies/Llei-11_2025-de-29-de-desembre-de-mesures-en-materia-dhabitatge-i-urbanisme.pdf

**Recommendation:** present temporary housing as a **scenario under study**, not a promise. Next step: get the consolidated
Llei 18/2007 after these 2025 changes from the Portal Jurídic (download by hand: the site blocks scripts). Then look for
any rule on temporary residential use of premises. NOMAD runs the same habitability checks either way: a temporary home
must still be liveable.
