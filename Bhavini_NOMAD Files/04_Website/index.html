Can an empty shop become homes?

    For the city · where to start

    Where can the city start?

    Every empty shop of the 2024 census and every unused warehouse, commercial or office building, sorted into three categories by what its zone allows and by street life. NOMAD gives no home counts here: how many homes fit needs a design and an architect.

    Use:

    Loading the city…

    Candidate: the zone allows homes and there is no avoid flag. Risky: the zone's own detailed plan decides, or "keep it a shop" (one of the 10 % of shops with the fewest shops within 100 m). Zoning doesn't allow: the zone takes no housing. Whole buildings have no street-life flag. In use or not: the 2024 census of ground-floor premises, a hint, not proof. Every place still needs its owner and a visit.

    Shops and offices already become homes, slowly

    Every number is copied from the source next to it. Official = the public body itself; secondary = press or a company quoting it: an order of magnitude, to confirm before relying on it.

    PlaceWhatNumberSource
    BarcelonaChanges of use shop → home granted, 2020-2025about 480 (and about 250 office → home)Metrópoli Abierta / El Español, from the city's works permits, 7 Jul 2026 · secondary

    EnglandPermitted development "Class MA" (commercial → residential): the council checks "adequate natural light in all habitable rooms" (MA.2(f)), as NOMAD checks daylightno floor-area cap since March 2024SI 2021/428 · official
    BarcelonaAPROP temporary homes (recycled containers) for families at risk, in their neighbourhood54 homes; Glòries: 42 homes, 26 weeks, €5.77 MAjuntament de Barcelona, Drets Socials · official · official

    What it means: Barcelona turns about 70 to 100 shops a year into homes (secondary figures) while 6,149 stand empty. The bottleneck is the process, not the stock: NOMAD says quickly which ones are worth starting, and which to avoid.
