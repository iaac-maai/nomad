2D iterations: old patio iterations vs circulation strategies

Asked 2 x 1-bed + 1 x 2-bed of every shop. The new iterations are circulation strategies, as Finch builds its options: flats on both façades, a corridor to the block interior (within 25 m of the street door), or small flats filling the shop. Homes counted only when they pass every rule. The old plans included flats lit only by a patio, which Decret 141/2012 Annex 1 3.6.1 does not accept as their façade (lab book E23).
