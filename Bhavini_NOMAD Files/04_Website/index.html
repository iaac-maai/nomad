      • 2 What you need

        a shop conversion needs 2,40 m (Decret 141/2012, Annex 4 group C)
        Kind of homes: Shared-space homes ("allotjaments amb espais comuns complementaris", Llei 18/2007 art. 18 bis): a fast, small-home route for people who need a home now. The whole shop stays one property. In Barcelona each home needs 40 m² of private space plus its share of the shared rooms. Density may be exceeded for homes for groups needing social attention (MPGM 2004 DA2). "Temporary protection housing" is only allowed on public land for facilities, not in a private shop.
        What matters most (weights)

        Like Finch's sliders: NOMAD tries many layouts and keeps, for each way of reaching the flats, the one that scores best. The four shares always add up to 100 %: raise one and the others make room.

        Your own plans

        Add a flat plan you drew: a DXF (Rhino: File > Export Selected) or JSON. Each room is a closed polyline on a layer named by its use (living, bedroom, kitchen, bath, hall, storage; or estar, dormitori, cuina, bany, rebedor). Doors and windows are lines across the opening, on layers "door" and "window". The flat's entrance door goes on "entrance", and rooms open to each other get a line on "open". NOMAD checks your plan like every real plan and furnishes it. If it passes, your layouts can use it, with its walls kept.

      • 3 Your layouts

        The shop as it is and its layouts appear here, one for each circulation strategy that adds homes: flats on both façades, a corridor to the block interior, or small flats filling the shop.

        Ask NOMAD

        Ask for changes in plain words, for example "make it all 2-bedroom flats", "favour more homes", or "in every flat, use the plan that stretched least". NOMAD's assistant changes the request, makes the layouts and swaps plans for you. It runs on your own Claude account.

      For the city · where to start

      Where can the city find homes?

      Every empty shop and every unused warehouse, commercial or office building in Barcelona was run through NOMAD: zoning, daylight (Bars's model where it exists), real plans, every rule. Set how many homes the city needs. NOMAD picks the fewest places that reach it and lists the ones to avoid.
